## Contributions

Unless you say otherwise, a contribution you submit is licensed as
MIT OR Apache-2.0, as in section 5 of the Apache License. Add
`Signed-off-by:` (`git commit -s`) to certify the Developer Certificate of
Origin (https://developercertificate.org/): you wrote it or have the right to
submit it under these licenses.

Do not copy code, documentation text, data tables or comments from GPL or
GFDL projects into the MIT/Apache parts. Reading them to
learn behaviour is fine; write the implementation and its text yourself.
Data should come from sources listed in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.

## Provenance

Parts of Forge were written with an AI assistant. Contributors should review
what they submit and make sure it contains nothing copied from a source with
an incompatible license.

The other plugins live in their own repositories (`forge-plugin-NAME`). Those that port GPL packages (consult, corfu, lsp, magit, markdown-mode, meow, vertico, bases, canvas, dashboard, graph, org-modern, rainbow-delimiters) are GPL-3.0-or-later; the rest are MIT OR Apache-2.0.
